# Ticket: Norvik Sable 2024

**SERVICE TICKET**

**CUSTOMER COMPLAINT**
"There's a grinding or scraping noise whenever I brake."

**TECHNICIAN FINDINGS**
Vehicle inspected and noise confirmed during braking test. Front brake pads checked through wheel spokes and found to be worn. Grinding noise indicates pad material has worn away, exposing metal backing.

**PART REPLACED**
Front brake pad set, part number BP-4421

**NOTES**
High priority safety repair. Worn brake pads reduce stopping effectiveness and can damage rotors if left unaddressed. Recommend rotor inspection at next service interval.
