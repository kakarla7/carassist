# Clicking sound but engine will not start

Symptom: Clicking sound but engine will not start.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Weak or dead battery
2. corroded battery terminals
3. failing starter

Quick checks: Check dashboard lights are bright; inspect terminals for white or green corrosion; try a jump-start.

Parts involved: Battery; Battery hold-down clamp. Part numbers differ by make, model, and year, so confirm the exact fit before ordering.

Safety: have it checked soon. It may leave you stranded or affect driving.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
