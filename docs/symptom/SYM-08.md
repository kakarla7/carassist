# One headlight is dim or out

Symptom: One headlight is dim or out.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Burned-out bulb
2. blown fuse

Quick checks: Swap in a known good bulb; check the headlight fuse.

Parts involved: Headlight bulb, low beam. Part numbers differ by make, model, and year, so confirm the exact fit before ordering.

Safety: have it checked soon. It may leave you stranded or affect driving.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
