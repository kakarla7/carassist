# Steering wheel shakes at highway speed

Symptom: Steering wheel shakes at highway speed.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Unbalanced wheels
2. worn tie rod
3. warped front rotors (if the shake is worse when braking)

Quick checks: Note speed range where shaking starts; check tire wear pattern.

Parts involved: Front brake rotor. Part numbers differ by make, model, and year, so confirm the exact fit before ordering.

SAFETY: this affects braking or steering. Do not keep driving normally; have it inspected promptly.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
