# Weak airflow from the vents

Symptom: Weak airflow from the vents.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Clogged cabin air filter
2. failing blower motor

Quick checks: Check cabin filter for dirt and debris; test all fan speeds.

Parts involved: Cabin air filter. Part numbers differ by make, model, and year, so confirm the exact fit before ordering.

Safety: low urgency, but fix it at the next service.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
