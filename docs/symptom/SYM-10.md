# Rattling from under the rear seat

Symptom: Rattling from under the rear seat.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Loose battery hold-down clamp

Quick checks: Lift the rear seat cushion and check whether the battery moves.

Parts involved: Battery hold-down clamp. Part number for these models: BAT-HD-47.

Safety: low urgency, but fix it at the next service.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
