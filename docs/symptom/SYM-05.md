# Check engine light is on and steady

Symptom: Check engine light is on and steady.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Loose gas cap
2. worn spark plugs
3. faulty oxygen sensor

Quick checks: Tighten gas cap and drive 20 miles; have codes read. A FLASHING light means stop driving.

Parts involved: Spark plug set. Part numbers differ by make, model, and year, so confirm the exact fit before ordering.

Safety: have it checked soon. It may leave you stranded or affect driving.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
