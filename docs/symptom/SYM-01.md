# Grinding or scraping noise when braking

Symptom: Grinding or scraping noise when braking.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Worn brake pads
2. scored rotors
3. stuck caliper

Quick checks: Check pad thickness through the wheel spokes; listen for noise only when braking.

Parts involved: Front brake pad set; Rear brake pad set; Front brake rotor. Part numbers differ by make, model, and year, so confirm the exact fit before ordering.

SAFETY: this affects braking or steering. Do not keep driving normally; have it inspected promptly.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
