# Wipers streak or squeak

Symptom: Wipers streak or squeak.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Worn wiper blades
2. dirty windshield

Quick checks: Inspect rubber edge for cracks; clean windshield and blades.

Parts involved: Front wiper blade set. Part numbers differ by make, model, and year, so confirm the exact fit before ordering.

Safety: low urgency, but fix it at the next service.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
