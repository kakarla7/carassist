# Car pulls to one side

Symptom: Car pulls to one side.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Uneven tire pressure
2. wheel alignment
3. stuck brake caliper

Quick checks: Check all four tire pressures; check whether one wheel is hot after driving.

Parts involved: Front brake pad set. Part numbers differ by make, model, and year, so confirm the exact fit before ordering.

Safety: have it checked soon. It may leave you stranded or affect driving.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
