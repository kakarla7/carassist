# Sluggish acceleration or slow response

Symptom: Sluggish acceleration or slow response.

Likely causes, most likely first:
1. Dirty engine air filter
2. worn spark plugs
3. fuel delivery issue

Quick checks: Inspect engine air filter for dirt; note whether the check engine light is on.

Parts involved: Engine air filter; Spark plug set. Part numbers differ by make, model, and year, so confirm the exact fit before ordering.

Safety: low urgency, but fix it at the next service.

A technician should confirm the diagnosis before parts are replaced.
