# Changing a tire: Arden Cora 2025

Tire size: 215/50R17. Recommended pressure: 35 psi.
The spare tire is stored here: Under trunk floor.

Model note: Battery is under the rear seat; lug nut torque raised to 90 ft-lb in 2025.

Steps:
1. Park on level ground, set the parking brake, and turn on the hazard lights.
2. Loosen the lug nuts about a half turn before raising the car.
3. Place the jack at the marked jack points: Marked notches behind front wheels and ahead of rear wheels.
4. Raise the car, remove the lug nuts, and swap the wheel.
5. Tighten the lug nuts by hand in a star pattern, then lower the car.
6. Torque the lug nuts to 90 ft-lb in a star pattern.
7. Check tire pressure and re-check the torque after about 50 miles.
