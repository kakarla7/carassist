# Maintenance schedule: Arden Brio 2024

Service schedule for the Arden Brio 2024:
- Engine oil and oil filter: every 7,500 miles. Oil filter: OF-9001.
- Cabin air filter: every 15,000 miles. Cabin air filter: CF-1201.

- Engine air filter: inspect at each oil change. Engine air filter: AF-6420.
- Wiper blades: replace when they streak. Front wiper blade set: WP-2400.
