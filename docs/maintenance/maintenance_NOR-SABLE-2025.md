# Maintenance schedule: Norvik Sable 2025

Service schedule for the Norvik Sable 2025:
- Engine oil and oil filter: every 7,500 miles. Oil filter: OF-9102.
- Cabin air filter: every 12,000 miles. Cabin air filter: CF-2306.

Model note: Cabin filter sits behind the glovebox; needs a trim removal tool.

- Engine air filter: inspect at each oil change. Engine air filter: AF-6430.
- Wiper blades: replace when they streak. Front wiper blade set: WP-2600.
