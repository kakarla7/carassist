# SB-2024-03-TERN: Tern and Vale front brake pads are not interchangeable

The Norvik Tern and Norvik Vale look alike but use different front brake pads. Tern: BP-4417. Vale: BP-4418. Check make, model, and year before ordering. Left mirror glass also differs: Tern MR-8801, Vale MR-8802.
