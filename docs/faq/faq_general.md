# FAQ: ordering the right part

Q: What do I need to find the right part?
A: The make, model, and model year. Part numbers can differ between similar models and between years.

Q: Can a technician confirm my diagnosis?
A: Yes. Symptom guides list likely causes, but a technician should confirm before parts are replaced.
