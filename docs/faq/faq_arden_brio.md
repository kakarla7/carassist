# FAQ: Arden Brio

Q: How often should I change the cabin air filter on the Arden Brio?
A: Every 15,000 miles.

Q: What oil does the Arden Brio use, and how much?
A: 0W-20, 4.5 liters, changed every 7,500 miles.

Q: What tire pressure should I use on the Arden Brio?
A: 36 psi for size 205/55R16.
