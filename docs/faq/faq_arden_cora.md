# FAQ: Arden Cora

Q: How often should I change the cabin air filter on the Arden Cora?
A: Every 15,000 miles.

Q: What oil does the Arden Cora use, and how much?
A: 0W-20, 4.8 liters, changed every 7,500 miles.

Q: What tire pressure should I use on the Arden Cora?
A: 35 psi for size 215/50R17.
