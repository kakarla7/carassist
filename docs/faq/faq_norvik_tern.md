# FAQ: Norvik Tern

Q: How often should I change the cabin air filter on the Norvik Tern?
A: Every 15,000 miles.

Q: What oil does the Norvik Tern use, and how much?
A: 0W-20, 4.5 liters, changed every 7,500 miles.

Q: What tire pressure should I use on the Norvik Tern?
A: 35 psi for size 225/55R17.
