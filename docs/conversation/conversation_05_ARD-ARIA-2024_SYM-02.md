# Conversation: Arden Aria 2024

Customer: Hey, so my Aria won't start. When I turn the key, I just hear this clicking noise over and over. Like click-click-click. What's going on?

Advisor: That's what I thought. Sounds like your battery is weak or dead. The clicking happens because there's not enough power to turn the engine over, but there's still a little juice left for the lights and that clicking sound.
