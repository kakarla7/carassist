# Conversation: Arden Aria 2025

Customer: Hey, so my Aria won't start. It's making this clicking sound over and over when I turn the key, but the engine just won't turn over. What's going on?

Advisor: I hear you—that clicking is usually a telltale sign. When you turn the key, are your dashboard lights coming on normally, or do they seem dim?

Customer: Now that you mention it, yeah, they're kind of dim.

Advisor: That points to a weak or dead battery. Check your battery terminals under the hood—if you see white or green crusty buildup on them, that's corrosion blocking the connection. You can try a jump-start first to confirm. If that works temporarily but the problem comes back, you'll need a new battery.

Customer: Okay, so I need a new battery?

Advisor: Most likely, yes. For your 2025 Aria with the 1.6L engine, you'll want a Battery, Group 48 AGM, part number BAT-48A. A dead battery won't damage anything, but don't let it fully drain repeatedly—it stresses the charging system. We can install it for you.
